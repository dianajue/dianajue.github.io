Academic writing is slow by design. A paper takes years from first idea to
print, and by the time it appears, the questions that made it interesting have
often moved on. This space is for the shorter half-formed things: what I am
reading, what surprised me in the field, and what I am still puzzling over.

Most of what shows up here will fall into three buckets:

- **Research notes** — findings that did not fit into a paper, or methods
  questions I keep running into.
- **Teaching** — what works and what does not in the strategy classroom.
- **Practice** — lessons from social enterprise that still shape the questions
  I ask.

If any of it is useful to you, that is a bonus. Mostly I am writing to think.
